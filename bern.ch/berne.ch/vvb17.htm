Berne - Little-known Facts

Did you know...?

That Einstein lived in Berne and developed his theory of relativity here? That the largest collection of Paul Klee's works is housed in Berne's Museum of Fine Art? That the world-famous Toblerone chocolate is made here? That the solar sail for American moon missions was designed at the University of Berne? That the largest stamp collection on view to the public can be seen at the Swiss Postal Museum?
