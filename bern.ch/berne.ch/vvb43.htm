Shopping in Berne

Longest shopping promenade in Europe- Leave your umbrella at home [picture, 42k]

With its 6 km/4 miles of arcades Berne can offer visitors one of the largest covered modern shopping promenades in a mediaeval setting in Europe.

One shop-window after another - an antique shop next to a boutique, a tobacconist's next to a confectioner's, a newsagent's kiosk next to a large department store, a jeweller's, a gallery, a fashion boutique, and here and there a restaurant, café, bar and so on - 6 km/4 miles in all!

Not to mention the street market (flowers/vegetables/fruit/meat and diary produce) which is held every Tuesday and Saturday morning in various squares and streets in the centre.
