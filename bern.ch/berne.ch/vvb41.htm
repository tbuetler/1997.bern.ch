Sightseeing in Berne

Arcades [picture, 23k]
With its 4 miles/6 kilometres of arcades, Berne boasts one of the largest covered shopping promenades in Europe, which makes this compact city on the Aare peninsula a paradise for shoppers and pedestrians.

Bear Pits [pictures, 35k]
Berne's heraldic animal. Since the late 15th century the bear pits have been situated at various locations in the city perimeters. 1513-1763 at Bärenplatz, now at the tip of the peninsula.

Botanical Gardens
Medicinal and fibre plants, examples of tropical and subtropical dry vegetation. Woodland and aquatic plants. Collection of plants from the cold steppes of Central Asia.

Cathedral [picture, 35k]
1421. Prime example of Swiss late-Gothic architecture. Last Judgement 1495 [picture, 52k]. Stained glass windows 1421-1450, carved choir stalls 1523. Steeple: highest spire in Switzerland (100 meters high).

Clock Tower [pictures, 33k]
Built in the early 13th century as a city gate, the historic Clock Tower is one of Berne's most famous landmarks and is open to visitors. Fascinating features include the figure-play and the astronomic clock built in 1530.

Dälhölzli, Zoological Gardens
Attractive woodland setting. Unique collection of European fauna, including Przewalski's horse, musk-ox, seals, European bison, elk, etc.

Fountains [picture, 16k]
11 historic fountains with allegorical statues (built around 1545) in the centre of the main streets.

French Church
1270-1285 Dominican Abbey Church. French-speaking Protestant Church since 1623. Oldest church in Berne.

Houses of Parliament [picture, 42k]
The Houses of Parliament are the seat of the Swiss Government (Federal Council) and of the Parliament (House of Representatives, Senate). A free guided tour will give visitors an insight into how the Swiss system of democracy works. During the four annual parliamentary sessions the debates can be followed from the gallery.

Markets [picture, 61k]
Tuesday and Saturday mornings are market days in Berne. This is where town and country meet. Farmers and market gardeners sell their fruit, vegetables and much more besides Parliament Square, and in nearby streets. In Münstergasse there is also the traditional meat and dairy produce market, and in December a Christmas market is held on Münsterplatz and Waisenhausplatz.

Prison Tower [picture, 23k]
Western gate of city 1256-1344. 2nd extension stage of city. Present tower built 1641-43 as a prison. Prison until 1897.

Rose Garden [picture, 42k]
Superb vantage point overlooking the whole of the old town.

Town Hall
1406-1417. Gothic secular architecture. Completely renovated 1939-42 when ground floor hall was rebuilt in original style and main floor redesigned. Guided tours restricted and only on request.
