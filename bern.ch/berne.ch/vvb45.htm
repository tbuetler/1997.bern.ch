Entertainment

Theatre Life: Berne's lively scene! [picture, 24k]
Theatre-goers are offered a wide range of attractions probably unequalled in any other European city of similar size. Berne is renowned for its Stadttheater as well as the city's many small theatres. Most are to be found in cellars in the old town quarter, where productions range from traditional theatre and the avant-garde to modern dance and mine.

And if you are a dancing enthusiast, then our international dancing festival - Berner Tanztage - is a "must".

The Music Scene [picture, 22k]
Berne's music scene, too, is extremely varied. The classical calendar features frequent performances by the Berne Symphony Orchestra and guest ensembles, as well as organ recitals in the Cathedral. Upon request, even special concerts can be arranged. Then there's the jazz scene in Berne which, incidentally, is the home of the Swiss Jazz School. Annual highlight is the International Jazz Festival, which takes place at the Kursaal at the end of April/beginning of May. Many other venues, too, offer jazz (e.g. in the Mahogany Hall), blues, folk and rock concerts, as well as performances by the Berne Troubadours.

Cinema
For programmes, see "Stadtanzeiger" and other daily newspapers. Showing normally in the afternoon and evening. Original-version movies with German and French subtitles.

Berne by night
A stroll under the arcades of Berne is not to be missed. Especially when the sights gleam under the streetlamps and the shop windows are enticingly lit. If you'd rather be more active, you can dance to the different music in Berne's dance-clubs and discotheques. The rich variety of Berne's music scene - from classical to tekkno - offers concerts by national and international artists. In Marian's Jazzroom, jazz enthusiasts can enjoy live performances by well-known jazz musicians. Some night-clubs and cabarets with international stars are also open till the small hours of the morning.
