Accommodation/Hotels

The hotels in the city and surrounding have approx. 3000 beds in various categories and offering various degrees of comfort. Most of the hotels are situated in the centre and, thanks to the fact that Berne its very compact, are within easy reach of the congress and exhibition facilities and sightseeing.

There are also a modern Youth Hostel (Weihergasse 4, below the Houses of Parliament, Tel. 311 63 16), and two camping sites in Berne.
