Museums and Libraries

Art

Museum of Fine Arts
World's largest Paul Klee collection. Important Johannes Itten Foundation. Interesting collection of 14th to 16th century Italian art and Swiss art from 15th century to present (Duccio, Fra Angelico, Picasso, Chagall, Kandinsky, Bill). French 19th and 20th-century artists and artists from Spain and Russia.

Art Gallery
Exhibitions of contemporary art.

Berne Collection of Plaster Replica
Plaster replica of ancient Greek and Roman sculptures.


History

Bernese Historical Museum
Swiss ethnographic collections, collections relating to Bernese history. Spoils of Burgundian campaign (famous tapestries). The original sculptures of the Last Judgement (Berne Cathedral). Everyday life past and present.

Swiss Rifle Museum
Comprehensive collection of firearms since 1817. Shooting trophies.


Natural History

Museum of Natural History
Europe's largest diorama: mammals and bird presented in their natural habitat. Splendid mineral collection. The original Barry, the famous Saint Bernard dog.


Miscellaneous Museums

Kornhaus
Former granary. Temporary exhibitions; incl. large collection of examples of 20th-century applied art.

Einstein House
Nobel prizewinner's apartment, where in 1905 he developed the principles of his revolutionary general theory of relativity.

Swiss Alpine Museum
This museum was completely renovated between 1990 and 1993 and provides, in four languages, an informative insight into the Swiss Alps. Special exhibits: 27 alpine topographical models, Hodler room, multivision, 10 audio-visual information stations, alpine data.

Museum of communication
One of the world's largest collections of postage stamps open to the public. Exhibition on the history of post and telecommunications.

Swiss Theatre Collection
Special library and documentation on Swiss theatre with permanent didactic exhibition on "Theatre Present and Past".

Swiss Literary Archives (in Swiss National Library)
Over 50 hand-written manuscripts by Swiss authors. Documentation on Swiss literature, library. Consultations only in reading room.

Museum of Psychiatry
On the campus of the Waldau University Clinic. The history of psychiatry at the Waldau Clinic.


Libraries

Swiss National Library
Documents the cultural, scientific and economic life of Switzerland past and present. Incorporating the Swiss Literary Archive with legacies and individual manuscripts of authors from the four linguistic regions of Switzerland.

Municipal and University Library
Central library of the University of Berne as well as the public library of the Canton and the City of Berne. Important collection of Bernese writing.

State Archives
Houses archives of the civic state of Berne pre- 1798.
