Gastronomy

The centre of Berne, including the Old Town, contains over 150 restaurants within a small area: cafés, historic cellar taverns (Klötzlikeller, Kornhauskeller), wine taverns, cosy old pubs, pizzerias and tea rooms. The pavement cafés are very popular with the Bernese as meeting-places. Whether you like Italian, French, Spanish, Chinese, American, Japanese or Swiss cuisine, there is something to meet all tastes.

Congress organizers who prefer to provide meals (especially lunch) on the congress premises will find all the necessary facilities at most congress halls. At every locality there are facilities for providing refreshments during a break.

For groups and particularly for incentive trips there is the interesting alternative of the Dine-Around System, by which guests receive a voucher which they can use in a variety of different restaurants.

Bernese Specialities

  • Bernese Onion Soup
  • Pea Soup with Ham
  • Berner Platte (boiled beef, pork, tongue, sausages and ham with sauerkraut and runner beans)
  • Berner Rösti (grated potato and onion fried in butter)
  • Burehamme (hot smoked ham)
  • Emmentaler lamb stew
  • Ratsherrentopf (Rösti with roast veal and beef, liver and sausage)
  • Onion tart
  • Meringues
  • Öpfelchüechli (hot apple doughnuts)
  • Züpfe (Bernese plaited bread)
  • Gingerbread
